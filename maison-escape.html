Fictional Concept

Maison Escape is a fictional travel and hospitality concept designed and developed by Soveren for showcase purposes only.

The Maison

Places worth
taking time for.

Maison Escape creates stays around a simple idea: the destination should never feel rushed.

From secluded coastlines to mountain houses, each property is imagined as a place to disconnect, slow down and experience its surroundings.
